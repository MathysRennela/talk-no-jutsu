# Anti-nitpicking guidance

## Potential signs of unproductive repetition
- Restates an objection already answered.
- Changes wording but not the underlying argument.
- Adds a hypothetical edge case with no plausible impact.
- Demands refinement without explaining its effect on the result.
- Reopens an issue without new evidence or changed assumptions.

These are signals to inspect the objection, not proof it is invalid.

## Intervention template
> This appears to repeat the concern recorded as [objection ID]. The previous
> response was [brief disposition]. What new evidence, changed assumption, or
> reasoning makes that response insufficient? If there is no material change,
> let's retain the existing disposition and continue.

## Safeguards
Do not label an objection nitpicking if it reveals a counterexample, shows
the previous response was wrong, changes the issue's importance, concerns
reproducibility or validity, or challenges an assumption now in question.

If no new information is supplied, do not spend another full round on the
same objection. Keep it visible. Critical unresolved issues remain blocking.
