# Example: conflicting scientific hypotheses

Schematic example, not a claim about a real result.

## Situation
- Agent A attributes an observation to mechanism X.
- Agent B attributes it to confounder Y.
- Agent C says current measurements cannot distinguish X and Y.

## Application
Record positions independently, including assumptions and evidence. Have A
reconstruct B's argument and vice versa. Diagnose the conflict as
interpretative and epistemic: the observation is shared, the causal
explanation is not.

Do not average the hypotheses. Design a measurement or intervention under
which X and Y make different predictions.

## Outcome
Observation: supported. Causal explanation: unresolved. Next action:
discriminating test. Preserve both hypotheses until new evidence is available.
