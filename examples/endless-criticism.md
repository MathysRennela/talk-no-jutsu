# Example: endless criticism

Schematic demonstration of the anti-nitpicking protocol.

Agent A proposes a method with a stated scope. Agent B raises a minor
boundary-case objection. A explains that the case is outside scope. B repeats
the concern in different wording.

A participant invokes Talk-no-Jutsu:

> This appears to be the same boundary-case objection recorded earlier.
> The response established that the case is outside the stated scope.
> Is there new evidence that the scope assumption is violated, or a
> counterexample within scope?

If B provides a new in-scope counterexample, reopen and investigate it.
If no new evidence or reasoning is supplied, retain the prior disposition
and move forward.

Anti-nitpicking requires repeated criticism to show what has changed; it is
not a defense against valid criticism.
