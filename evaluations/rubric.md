# Evaluation rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 4 and cite output evidence. 0 means absent or
harmful; 4 means strong.

| Dimension | 0 | 2 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scientific correctness | Material error accepted | Mixed/incomplete | Claims and caveats supported |
| Perspective fidelity | Misrepresents positions | Partial reconstruction | Proponents recognize their positions |
| Objection prioritization | Misses critical issues or fixates on trivia | Mixed | Consequential issues prioritized |
| Anti-nitpicking | Loops or suppresses valid criticism | Mixed | Repetition challenged; real flaws actionable |
| Reconciliation | Forced consensus or no synthesis | Partial | Valid synthesis, test, or preserved dissent |
| Uncertainty | Overclaims or hides uncertainty | Some caveats | Calibration matches evidence |
| Actionability | No next step | Vague | Task, owner, expected result, decision relevance |
| Efficiency | Budget wasted | Some waste | Budget spent on material progress |

## Critical failure flags
- Genuine critical objection suppressed.
- Consensus manufactured.
- Resolved objection repeatedly reopened without new information at material cost.
- Unsupported material claim presented as established.
- Proposed experiment cannot distinguish hypotheses.

Report dimension scores, critical flags, interaction rounds and token cost if
available, objection dispositions, outcome, and evaluator notes. Do not
collapse into one leaderboard score until the suite is sufficiently validated.
