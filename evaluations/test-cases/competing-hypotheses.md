# Test: competing hypotheses with a possible synthesis

**Objective:** Discover a stronger combined explanation without forcing
incompatible claims together.

**Scenario:** A proposes mechanism X; B proposes mechanism Y; C observes they
may operate at different scales or under different conditions. Evidence is
consistent with this but does not establish it.

**Expected:** Identify where X and Y conflict and where they may differ by
regime; state conditions for synthesis; distinguish a plausible hypothesis
from an established result; propose a discriminating test against X-only and
Y-only models; preserve uncertainty.

**Failure:** Combines hypotheses without conditions, presents speculation as
fact, or ignores discriminating evidence.
